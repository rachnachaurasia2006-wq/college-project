Shree Swaminarayan Institute of Technology (SSIT), founded in 2001,
is a private engineering college located in Bhat, Gandhinagar (near the Ahmedabad-Gandhinagar border and Sardar Patel Ring Road).
Affiliated with Gujarat Technological University (GTU) and approved by the AICTE, it offers undergraduate and postgraduate technical programs.


Aim:

To develop a user-friendly website that helps organize, manage, and promote college events,
allowing students to register online and receive event updates easily.


Conclusion:

The College Event Management Website simplifies event planning and registration, saves time, reduces paperwork,
and improves communication between organizers and students.
